How to Interpret the 5x5 Risk Severity Matrix
Each identified risk is evaluated across Probability (1 to 5) and Impact (1 to 5). The Severity Score = Probability × Impact (range: 1 to 25). This standardizes enterprise risk management across technical, schedule, dependency, and resource categories.
High Severity (Score 15–25) Critical risks with severe timeline or architectural impact. Immediate mitigation plan and executive escalation required.
Medium Severity (Score 8–14) Moderate risks requiring active management, sprint allocation, and assigned engineering owners.
Low Severity (Score 1–7) Minor operational risks tracked in standard backlog reviews without immediate schedule threat.
